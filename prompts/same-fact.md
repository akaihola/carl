# Question

Two web searches each wrote a draft card, `card_1` and `card_2`, to correct
the claim in `candidate`, or to answer its open question. `date_time` says
when the table is. Do the two cards state the same fact? The cards may be in
different languages (Finnish or English); judge them as they are.

# Choices

- `same fact`: both state the same corrected fact or the same answer, even
  in different words or languages.
- `compatible but different`: both can be true at once, but they state
  different facts.
- `contradict`: they can't both be true (1954 against 1955).
