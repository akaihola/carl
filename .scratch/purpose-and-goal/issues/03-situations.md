# Situations Carl serves

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

In which situations is Carl used: where, with whom, how many speakers, on which device and screen, and in which language(s) (Finnish, English, mixed)?

## Answer

Settled with the owner in a grilling session.

- **Setting:** built around dinner or coffee at home. Restaurants, cafés and informal work lunches are supported but not optimised for. Video calls and cars are out.
- **People:** the owner plus 1–5 adults they know well. Children may be present but aren't a design target.
- **Speakers:** 2–6, usually 3–4; more than 6 is best-effort. Carl only needs to tell speakers apart, not identify them.
- **Device:** the owner's phone, propped up so everyone at the table can see it. No second screen; wearables are out (they would need an app).
- **Language:** Finnish and English both count fully. The language can switch between utterances and English loanwords appear in Finnish speech. Fact cards are written in the conversation's main language. No other languages.
- **Session:** 30 min to 2 h, left running in the background for the whole conversation.
- **Acoustics:** one device with its built-in microphone, 0.5–2 m from the speakers, with normal household background noise. Combining several devices is out.
